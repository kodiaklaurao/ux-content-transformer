# Kodiak Hub Glossary


**Properties:** All the information about suppliers available on Kodiak Hub. Properties are configurable to fit most use cases. These also will help to segment the supplier base by adding tags for business areas or any other needed criteria.

**Category tree:** As an additional way to segment suppliers on the platform, a category tree can be created to assign suppliers to the different categories.

**Attributes:** Data points on which you'll be able to rate the suppliers. Attributes is the Kodiak Hub way to name questions and their answers, either asked to or for your suppliers.

**sign(UP):** Our supplier self-registration module. Suppliers can register through a configurable portal, streamlining the start of the onboarding process.

**in(SITE):** Our supplier assessment module. Through it, you'll be able to collect information directly from your suppliers. This can be used for example for self-assessments or onboarding purposes.

**Assessment:** It qualifies the questionnaires sent out to the suppliers.

**on(SITE):** Our auditing module. You can create templates and prepare audits. This module is also tied to an app, which can be used on the field by auditors.

**kp(INSIGHT):** Our supplier performance evaluation module. Create your own acceptance criteria, and request the evaluation from any stakeholders required, internal or external.

**im(PROVE):** The hub for actions. This serves as an active collaboration platform with suppliers or internal stakeholders, where updated documentation can be requested, or an improvement plan in multiple steps can be shaped.

**Template:** Prepare questionnaires in our three core modules — **in(SITE)**, **on(SITE)**, and **kp(INSIGHT)** — to guarantee all necessary information leading to an evaluation is collected. These templates are used in campaigns.

**Campaign:** The grouped send-out of questionnaires in our three core modules.

**Reports:** All the answers and ratings on submitted assessments, audits, and evaluations.

**safe(SOURCE):** Our country rating. This pulls together data from 39 approved indexes to give you a first overview of the risks of the countries suppliers are located in. Additionally, a country financial rating is also available to complete the picture.

**trade(TRUST):** Our sanctions and politically exposed persons (PEP) screening module. It screens suppliers daily against global sanctions lists, watchlists, and PEP databases, and updates automatically when a supplier's risk status changes.

**media(INSIGHT):** A third-party service that provides a news feed and adverse media on the selected supplier. It updates automatically, and historical data can be requested.

**fin(SIGHT):** A third-party financial rating. European suppliers can be checked thanks to their VAT or Company Registration Number (only for UK).

**Intelligence:** Our analytics module. Visualizes and analyzes supplier data across ratings, assessments, and performance through configurable reports and charts.

**Goal Setting:** Defines a value-based rating methodology — on a 0–100% scale — that sets acceptance criteria and benchmarks for supplier performance.

**Smart Actions:** Automatically triggers supplier actions based on predefined events, such as document expiration, rating drops, or new supplier onboarding.

**Document AI Intelligence:** Uses AI to classify uploaded documents and extract structured data fields, with manual override and confidence indicators.

**Analytics 2.0:** A next-generation analytics layer with out-of-the-box interactive reports, advanced filtering, and configurable chart types for procurement decision-making.

**Supplier scorecard:** Your overview compiling all information about the supplier. The scorecard will combine the ratings emanating from the different modules and third-party services, and include any collaborative action taken with the supplier.

**Rating areas:** These are defined areas to separate a supplier's rating. Developed with the Stockholm School of Economics, this enables the comparison of suppliers' compliance and/or performance by comparable criteria.

**Products & Services:** Additional scope on the platform to view which suppliers sell commodities tied to produced and/or sold products.

**User management:** Administration of the user type/access level of people with access to the platform. This part is only visible and accessible to superusers (administrators).
